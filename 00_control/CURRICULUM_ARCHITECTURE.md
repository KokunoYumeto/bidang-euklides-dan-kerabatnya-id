Selected 2026-08-21. Translation completion and curriculum selection are
separate decisions; the already admitted Indonesian Petrunin edition remains
the sole external instructional spine because it independently passed the

## Three required layers

1. **Complete Petrunin spine.** Retain every chapter from metric preliminaries
   through area, all 253 exercises, all 247 linked current-master hints, all
   figures, formulas, identifiers, references, citations, indexes and the
   author's warning that Chapters 14-20 are not always fully rigorous.
2. **Original companion.** Separately author and attribute the CC BY-SA 4.0
   companion *Transformations, Invariants, and Model Surfaces*. It supplies
   prerequisites; the Erlangen comparison grammar and invariant table;
   rigorous affine/projective closure; spherical, Poincare and Klein model
   comparison; the minimum surface/quotient/orientability/Euler
   characteristic/geodesic/curvature/Gauss-Bonnet bridge; and two
   cross-geometry capstones. It is not by Petrunin. Each retained load-bearing
   Chapter 14-20 claim must be proved, explicitly assumed, or excluded from a
   mastery claim.
3. **Original solutions/mastery volume.** Separately author and attribute this
   CC BY-SA 4.0 volume. Preserve all 247 author hints. For every source and
   companion exercise add prerequisites, difficulty/misconception tags,
   staged hints, an answer/check and a complete worked proof/solution. Add 20
   chapter checks, four cumulative checks, two capstones, keys, rubrics and
   dependency warnings.

Semantic backend plus accessible HTML/EPUB are required release layers, not
additional print texts. GeoGebra is optional external enrichment only. No
second whole textbook or external excerpt is required course reading.
